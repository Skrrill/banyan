The honest price

Why it is $11, not $40.

A fair price should not feel suspicious. Here is exactly how we keep it low without cutting a single corner on the teaching.

No middlemen

You reach the teacher directly. No sales floor, no call centre, no layers taking a cut of every class you pay for.

No package trap

We do not need you locked into 96 classes to survive. You pay per class, so we only earn if you keep choosing to stay.

Teachers paid well

Low price does not mean underpaid teachers. It means the money goes to teaching, not to advertising and overhead. Our teachers are paid properly.

Same price, everywhere on earth. A family in Ohio and a family in Chennai pay the identical $11. Most platforms quietly charge each country a different rate. We publish one number and never move it.
